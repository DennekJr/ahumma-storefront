# Ahumma Product Strategy

## Brand focus

### Premium natural body care for melanin-rich skin

Ahumma creates luxurious, everyday body care that nourishes, protects and celebrates healthy melanin-rich skin through simple, effective rituals.

## Product focus — launch collection

The launch portfolio is intentionally focused on products with high repeat-purchase potential.

### Core categories

- Lightweight body butters
- Liquid African black soap

## Product philosophy

Every Ahumma product should be:

- Deeply nourishing
- Lightweight and fast-absorbing
- Suitable for everyday use
- Designed for dry to combination skin
- Rich in naturally derived ingredients
- Sensory, luxurious and enjoyable to use

# Claims and formulation checks

Before using this strategy as public-facing copy, confirm each claim against the final formula, label, testing and regulatory guidance. In particular, review:

- “Lightweight” and “fast-absorbing”
- “Suitable for sensitive skin”
- “Healthy-aging body care”
- “Clarifying”
- “Removing impurities”
- “Long-lasting hydration”
- “Naturally derived” ingredient language
- Fragrance notes and ingredient lists

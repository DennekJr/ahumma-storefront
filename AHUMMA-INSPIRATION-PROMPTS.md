# Ahumma Inspiration Prompt Library

Use this library to study and adapt useful patterns from:

- Arami Essentials: https://aramiessentials.com/
- Aesop: https://www.aesop.com/

These are reference brands, not templates to reproduce. Ahumma must keep its own Nigerian origin, premium body-care focus, and specific positioning for Black and brown skin.

## Research guardrails

Every prompt in this library must follow these rules:

1. Inspect the current Ahumma implementation before recommending or changing anything.
2. Compare Ahumma with both reference brands where the relevant experience is publicly available.
3. Separate verified observations, reasonable inferences, and recommendations.
4. Do not copy branded wording, product names, distinctive layouts, proprietary photography, logos, or code.
5. Adapt principles and interaction patterns into Ahumma's existing visual system and architecture.
6. Preserve the working positioning: Nigerian-rooted premium body care for Black and brown skin everywhere.
7. Keep copy punchy, warm, sensory, culturally grounded, and globally relevant. Avoid generic wellness language, clinical overclaiming, and repeated use of “ritual” as filler.
8. Reuse existing Ahumma assets and project conventions before proposing new dependencies or production work.
9. Return exact file paths for every implementation recommendation.
10. If a reference page cannot be inspected, say so and do not invent page-specific findings.

## What Ahumma can borrow

- Clear delivery and location context without hiding the global proposition.
- Product discovery organized around real customer needs, not only product names.
- Short, sensory product writing that makes repetition feel desirable.
- Editorial pacing: strong images, deliberate whitespace, and fewer competing messages.
- A concise philosophy statement that explains what makes the care different.
- Story, ingredient, gifting, and service paths that support both discovery and trust.
- An inline newsletter with a clear reason to join.
- A separate, respectful promotional popup with persistence and accessibility rules.
- Reuse, refill, pickup, stockist, or gifting features only when Ahumma's operations genuinely support them.
- A calm, useful utility layer: search, currency, delivery, contact, and help.

## What Ahumma should not copy

- Exact headlines, slogans, product descriptions, campaign language, or email language.
- Logos, photography, videos, illustrations, layouts, or distinctive visual assets.
- Reference-specific color systems or typography treated as Ahumma's identity.
- Claims about sustainability, refill, sourcing, efficacy, or operations that Ahumma cannot verify.
- A generic “for everyone” position that weakens the Black and brown skin focus.
- Excessive minimalism that removes product education or Nigerian context.
- Luxury cues that make the site feel cold, inaccessible, or disconnected from the customer.
- Features added only because a reference brand has them, without catalogue, operational, or data support.

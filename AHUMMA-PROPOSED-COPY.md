# Ahumma Proposed Website Copy

This document preserves the proposed Ahumma website copy and structure supplied for review. The customer-facing version should stay close to this language. SEO and AI-search enhancements are included separately so they can be evaluated without making the main experience feel over-written.

## Working brand tone

- Punchy, warm and culturally grounded.
- Premium without sounding clinical, corporate or overly polished.
- Lead with feeling and memorable phrases; use the supporting line for clarity.
- Speak specifically to Black and brown skin rather than broadening the audience to “everyone.”
- Make Nigerian origin and global ambition feel confident, not defensive or over-explained.
- Prefer short sentences, strong nouns and sensory language.
- Use “ritual” as a meaningful care behavior, not as decoration in every section.
- Avoid generic wellness language, correction-focused language and long explanatory paragraphs.

# Claims to verify before publishing

The following wording should be checked against product formulations, regulatory guidance, shipping capability and approved brand claims before it goes live:

- “Clean” and “natural” as blanket product claims
- “Plant-based” for every product
- “Authentic” African black soap
- “Sensitive skin” suitability
- “Hypoallergenic”
- “Newborn skin” suitability
- “Antibacterial” or “clarifying” claims
- “Targets blemishes” or “soothes irritation”
- “Skin-compatible pH”
- “Ethically sourced” ingredients
- “Fast international shipping”
- U.S. shipping availability and delivery promises
- Free delivery threshold and currency values
- “Eczema” as a consultation-form concern

Use the simpler primary copy wherever a claim cannot yet be substantiated.

## 13. FAQ

### Frequently Asked Questions About Ahumma

**What is Ahumma?**

Ahumma is a Nigerian-born premium body-care brand for Black and brown skin, rooted in African heritage. We create body-care products including whipped body butters and liquid African black soap, designed to make everyday skincare feel beautiful and nourishing.

**Is Ahumma a Black-owned African beauty brand?**

Ahumma is a Nigerian-born African beauty brand created with African heritage at its heart. The brand celebrates Black and brown skin and draws inspiration from African ingredients, rituals and beauty traditions.

**What products does Ahumma make?**

Ahumma currently creates premium body butters and liquid African black soap. Our body-care collection includes Sika, Dream Whip and Baby Bloom body butters, alongside Ara Liquid African Black Soap.

**What skin types are Ahumma body butters suitable for?**

Ahumma body butters are created to nourish and moisturise the body, making them especially suited to skin that feels dry, rough or in need of richer moisture. Our products are designed with Black and brown skin in mind.

**What ingredients are used in Ahumma body butters?**

Depending on the formula, Ahumma body butters feature nourishing ingredients including shea butter, mango butter, cocoa butter, jojoba oil and avocado oil.

**What is African black soap?**

African black soap is a traditional African cleansing soap associated with ingredients such as plantain or cocoa pod ash and nourishing oils. Ahumma's Ara brings this heritage into a convenient liquid format for modern everyday body care.

**Where is Ahumma made?**

Ahumma is a Nigerian-born brand with roots in Lagos, Nigeria. Our products and brand philosophy are inspired by African ingredients, beauty traditions and heritage.

**Does Ahumma ship to the United States?**

Yes. Ahumma is building for a global audience, with shipping available across Nigeria and the United States, alongside international destinations as global distribution grows.

**Is Ahumma for Black and brown skin?**

Yes. Black and brown skin is at the heart of Ahumma. We create body-care rituals that celebrate melanin-rich skin and encourage people to nourish, care for and enjoy the skin they are in.

**Does Ahumma believe in skin correction?**

No. Ahumma was founded on the belief that you are enough and beautiful by design. We don't believe in fixing you. We believe in caring for you—creating body care that nourishes your skin rather than treating beauty as something you need to earn.
